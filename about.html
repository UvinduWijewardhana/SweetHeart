About SweetHeart

SweetHeart is a growing Sri Lankan project for sweet, personal moments. You can create a playful date invitation link today, and birthday cards, wedding invitations and a gift shop are on the way.

How it works

  • Make it yours. Enter your WhatsApp number, their name, an optional nickname, and choose the date ideas they can pick from. You can add your own places too.
  • Send the link. Preview it first, then share it however you like.
  • They answer. They choose a place, a day and a time, and tap a button that opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message to you.

What we believe

An invitation should always leave room for a real answer, and “no” is a perfectly good answer. SweetHeart is meant for fun, respectful invitations — never for pressuring anyone. Please read our Terms.

Your privacy

There are no accounts and no database. The details you enter live inside the link you create, and SweetHeart does not store them on a server. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Who we are

SweetHeart is an independent project made in Sri Lanka. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta or Google. Questions or ideas? Get in touch.
