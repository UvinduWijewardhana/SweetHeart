Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use SweetHeart. We have tried to keep it short and honest.

1. What we save when you create an invitation

When you create a Date Invite or an Employee Appreciation link, the details you enter (names, a nickname, the places or the gift, the date and time, a short message, your WhatsApp number, and any small photo or logo you add) are saved in our database, which is hosted by Supabase. We save them only so that the person who opens your link can see your invitation.

How long we keep them: a Date Invite is deleted automatically after 24 hours. An Employee Appreciation invitation is kept until the day after the date you booked (never longer than 90 days) and is then deleted automatically. After that the link stops working. Photos and logos are saved as small square pictures and are deleted together with the invitation.

If the database cannot be reached, the site may instead pack the details into a long link inside your own browser. Then nothing is saved on our side, but anyone who has the long link can decode what is in it.

Please note: anyone who has your link can see what is in it, including your WhatsApp number, so only share it with the person you are inviting. You can ask us to delete an invitation before it expires by emailing us the link.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

If you report an invitation, we save the invitation's code, the reason and note you choose, and a scrambled marker of your connection for up to 90 days, so that we can review it and stop abuse. Reports are anonymous to the person who made the invitation.

Your browser may also store your language choice and, if you try the gift shop preview, your cart, in its local storage on your own device. This stays on your device.

2. The invitation page and WhatsApp

When someone opens an invitation link, the page shows the saved details. When they finish and tap the WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message to the number in the invitation. The message is only sent if they press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from that point.

3. Hosting and technical data

The website is served by a third-party host (currently GitHub Pages) and saved invitations are kept with Supabase. Like most services, they may log technical data such as IP addresses. To limit spam we also save a scrambled marker of your connection (not your real IP address) together with the invitation, and it is deleted with it.

4. Fonts

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. This means your browser requests files from Google's servers, so Google can see your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies and advertising

SweetHeart itself does not set cookies. We may show advertisements on some pages using Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), we will ask for your consent before showing personalised ads.

Invitation pages, the ones the invited person sees, are meant to stay free of advertising.

6. Children

SweetHeart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your choices

Saved invitations are deleted automatically (see section 1). To have one deleted sooner, or to ask what we hold, write to uvinduwije@gmail.com and include the link.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. When accounts, orders and payments launch, we will update this policy before we collect any such information.
